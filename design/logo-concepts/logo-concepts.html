Vashon Software · logo concepts · draft 1

Four marks to compare

Each concept is shown large on a light and a dark background, then at 16, 24, 32 and 64 px (favicon to header size), next to the name, and in one flat color. Every mark is a flat vector, so it prints, embroiders and stamps in one color. The current gold-ball "VS" can't do that.

1. Island Node

Earlier pick

Vashon and Maury drawn as one silhouette (the narrow Portage neck and Quartermaster Harbor between them), with four mesh-radio nodes linked across it. The cedar node marks the hub. It says "Vashon" and "connected systems" in one mark and fits the emergency, mesh and architecture work.

2. Reflected Fir V

A Douglas fir reflected in the Sound. Below the waterline its stepped tiers form a V. It's the most typographic option and makes the strongest monogram, but it says less about the work.

3. Contour Wave

Three topo lines that rise to a peak (a hill, a tree or a V) and flatten into water at the edges. The cedar dot works as a summit marker or a signal. It's calm and map-like, nods to GIS and emergency mapping, and is the least literal of the four.

4. Island Tile

Concept 1 on a rounded square with the links removed, for favicons, app icons and social avatars, where the full node pattern is too small to see. It pairs with concept 1, not instead of it.
